ABOUT ME

很多人问我，为什么偏爱原版英文绘本。

于我而言，并不是为了学习一门语言。

每一门语言都有它独有的声音节奏与美感。英文绘本里的抑扬顿挫，是故事的一部分；中文文字里含蓄绵长的意境，同样动人。

我既是陪伴孩子梳理情绪的心理咨询工作者，也是一名绘本的分享者。

我希望在这里，借由绘本故事，安放养育里的困惑。家长可以依自己和孩子当下的心境，自由选择阅读的语言版本，单纯享受共读本身带来的联结与感动。

Many people ask why I love original English picture books. For me, it has nothing to do with language learning. Every language carries its own unique rhythm and beauty. The cadence of English words becomes part of the story itself; the subtle, enduring warmth of Chinese prose moves us in its own unmistakable way.

I am a counselor who walks with children through their emotions, and a story sharer. My goal here is to ease the stress of parenting through picture books. You can feel free to choose whatever reading language fits your family's mood, and simply savor the bond and warmth you share when you read together.
